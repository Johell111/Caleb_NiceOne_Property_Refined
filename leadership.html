Managing Director

Caleb Odukoya

Caleb Odukoya leads Caleb NiceOne Property with a clear purpose: to help people make confident property decisions and create lasting value through real estate.

As Managing Director, he guides the company’s strategy and day-to-day operations, including business development, property marketing, client relationships and investment initiatives. His approach brings together professional service, transparent communication and careful attention to each client’s goals.

Caleb believes property ownership and investment can help individuals and families build financial security. That belief shapes the company’s work with buyers, investors, property owners and developers, and its commitment to dependable guidance from the first conversation through to completion.

Leadership philosophy

Integrity and trust guide Caleb’s decisions. He is focused on building a respected real estate company known for responsible practice, strong relationships and sustainable value.

His commitment

  • Clients: Clear guidance and professional service.
  • Investors: Credible opportunities and informed decisions.
  • Property owners: Effective marketing and professional representation.
  • The industry: Integrity, excellence and responsible practice.
Strong relationships grow through trust, consistency and a genuine commitment to clients.

Executive Director

Eunice Odukoya

Eunice Odukoya helps turn Caleb NiceOne Property’s vision into the thoughtful, dependable service clients experience every day.

As Executive Director, she supports strategic planning and business growth while strengthening operations, client relationships and service delivery. She brings professionalism, dedication and close attention to detail to her work with the management team.

Eunice understands that property decisions carry both financial and personal weight. She is committed to making each interaction clear, respectful and reliable, so clients can move through their real estate journey with greater confidence.

Leadership philosophy

Her approach is grounded in teamwork, integrity and continuous improvement. She works to build the strong systems and lasting relationships that help the company serve clients consistently.

Her commitment

  • Clients: Courteous, dependable and professional service.
  • Partners: Relationships built on trust, transparency and shared value.
  • The company: Sound operations and sustainable growth.
  • The community: Support for responsible property ownership and investment.
